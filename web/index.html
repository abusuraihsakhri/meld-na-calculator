Methods and limits

  • Inputs below 1 are floored to 1 for bilirubin, INR and creatinine.
  • Sodium is bounded to 125–137 mEq/L. MELD 3.0 albumin is bounded to 1.5–3.5 g/dL.
  • Creatinine is capped at 3 mg/dL for MELD 3.0 or 4 mg/dL for historical scores; qualifying dialysis forces the relevant cap.
  • Scores are rounded to whole numbers and bounded to 6–40.
  • No mortality prediction or exception-point eligibility is inferred from these scores.
